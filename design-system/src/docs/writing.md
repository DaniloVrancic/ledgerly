# Writing

Plain, calm and precise. No guilt, no hype. Ledgerly states what is true and what can be done next.

## Voice

- **Plain.** Use everyday words. Say "Spending", not "Expenditure".
- **Calm.** Going over a budget is a fact, not a failure.
- **Precise.** Give the number and the date.
- **Short.** One sentence where one will do. No exclamation marks.

## Example wording

| Moment | Write | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Empty state | Nothing here yet. Transactions you add will appear here, grouped by day. | You haven't added anything! Start your journey today |
| Budget warning | Food is at 89%, with €42.94 left for 2 days. | Careful! You're about to blow your budget |
| Over limit | Shopping is €24.89 over its limit. | You overspent again |
| Backup failure | Backup failed. Ledgerly could not write to your backup folder. Your data is safe on this phone. | Error 500: backup failure |
| Estimate label | Estimate. Based on your balance, upcoming bills and normal daily spending. | Guaranteed safe to spend |
| Undo | Transaction deleted. Undo | Are you sure? |
| Import failure | This file could not be read. It may be damaged. Nothing in Ledgerly was changed. | Import failed |

## Empty-state style

Every empty state has the same parts: the line ledger with one icon rising out of it, a short heading, one sentence, and at most one action. No full-colour illustrations or mascots. A cleared Needs Review list is a calm statement of fact: "All reviewed."

## Definitions

Show these words wherever the metric appears, in a tooltip.

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Balance | Sum of all account balances. Credit cards count as negative. |
| Income | Income transactions only. |
| Spending | Expenses minus refunds. |
| Net | Income minus spending. |
| Savings rate | Net divided by income. |
