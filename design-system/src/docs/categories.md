# Custom categories

People can add their own categories, with any icon they want. Each category belongs to accounts.

## What a category holds

- **Name.** Up to 32 characters, including č, ć, đ, š and ž.
- **Type.** Expense or Income.
- **Icon.** Anything in the line set, or the person's own image. An image is cropped to a square, resized to about 96px and stored with the attachments. It always sits beside the name, because an image brings no guarantee of contrast.
- **Colour.** One of the twelve curated colours. Colour stays curated so that chips stay readable.
- **Accounts.** All accounts by default, or the accounts chosen. At least one is needed.

## How accounts change the app

- The Add sheet shows only the categories that belong to the selected account. Changing the account pill changes the chips, and the All chip follows the same rule.
- History, budgets and reports always keep a category, whichever account it belongs to. Changing its accounts later never moves or hides past entries.
- A category with no account limit appears for every account.
- An account with few categories still shows Later, so an entry can always be saved.

## Where it lives

The Categories screen sits under More. It lists categories by type and can filter by account. A new category opens as a sheet, so it can also be created from the All chip on the Add sheet without leaving the flow.

## Rules

- Offer the whole icon set. Do not limit icons to the built-in categories.
- Show a live preview of the tile, name and accounts at the top of the form.
- Mark a person's own categories with the label Yours.
- Categories in use cannot be destroyed. Deleting one still needs its entries moved, or it can be archived.
